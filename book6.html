About the Book

Blood Beneath the Moon: Beyond the Bloodlines is a gripping dark fantasy romance where forbidden love, ancient prophecies, and supernatural bloodlines collide. As war brews between vampires, werewolves, and witches, three unlikely souls discover that the fate of their world has always been written in their blood.

Synopsis

Beneath the surface of ordinary life, vampires, werewolves, and witches conceal a centuries-old war born from bloodshed, betrayal, and a prophecy that was never truly buried. Camille Blackthorne, an immortal vampire descended from an ancient bloodline thought lost to history, has spent decades hiding from a past she never fully understood.

Ravi Brooks, a young werewolf carrying the legacy of two powerful lineages, struggles against instincts and power that grow more dangerous with every passing moon. And Esperanza “Ezzy” Ortega, a witch haunted by violent visions she cannot control, begins uncovering truths tied to a coven whose magic once changed the world forever.

Their lives were never meant to intertwine, yet something older than memory continues pulling them together. As strange visions bleed into reality and buried magic begins awakening beneath the coming Blood Moon, the three descendants find themselves tied to the remnants of an ancient tragedy involving the first hybrid: Ahuli Riverwind. A name feared by vampires, hidden from wolves, and carried through generations in secret.

While a fragile treaty between vampires and werewolves begins collapsing into chaos, powerful elders and hidden covens move from the shadows to stop history from repeating itself. But the deeper Camille, Ravi, and Ezzy fall into the truth of their bloodlines, the more they realize the prophecy was never about one of them alone. Ravi may be the vessel, Camille the final surviving tether to a cursed lineage, and Ezzy the heir to the forbidden magic that created it all.

As war spreads beneath moonlit skies and the Blood Moon draws closer, love becomes tangled with destiny, memories with instinct, and sacrifice with survival. Because if the prophecy is fulfilled, the world may witness the rebirth of something that should have died centuries ago.
